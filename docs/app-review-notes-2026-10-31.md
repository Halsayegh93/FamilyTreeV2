1. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Al-Mohammad Ali Family is a private family network for the members of the Al-Mohammad Ali extended family (Kuwait and abroad). It solves a real problem for large extended families: keeping an accurate, up-to-date family tree, knowing who is related to whom, and sharing family news (births, weddings, condolences), gatherings (diwaniyas) and family projects in one trusted place instead of scattered WhatsApp groups. Members can browse the interactive family tree, find the kinship path between any two members, read and post family news, and keep their own branch of the tree up to date. Every new account is reviewed by the family administrators before it can see family data, which protects members' privacy.

3. ACCOUNT REGISTRATION AND DELETION
- Registration: a new user enters a phone number, verifies the SMS code, and fills a short join form (name and father). The request waits for approval by the family administrators. While waiting, the user can still delete the account from the waiting screen.
- Deletion: Profile > Settings > Delete Account. This sends a deletion request. It is carried out within 7 days at most. The user's phone number, email, photo, posts and comments are deleted. Only the person's name and position in the family tree remain, because they are part of other members' family records.

4. USER-GENERATED CONTENT SAFEGUARDS
- Users must accept the Terms of Use (zero tolerance for objectionable content) before using the app.
- Every post and comment has Report and Block options. Blocking a member hides their content immediately.
- Reports go to the administrators. Approving a report removes the content, and administrators act on reports within 24 hours.
- Comments pass through a server-side filter for objectionable words.
- News posts require administrator approval before they are published.

5. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Supabase (supabase.com): database, authentication, file storage and realtime updates.
- kwtSMS (kwtsms.com): sends the SMS one-time login code.
- Apple Push Notification service: notifications.
- Apple Maps (link only): opens diwaniya locations.
- The app has no ads, no analytics SDKs, no AI services, no payments and no in-app purchases. All features are free.

6. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app works the same in all regions, in Arabic and English.
